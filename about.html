I fall for things easily. A place, an idea, a feeling. And once I do, I go all the way in.

Curious-Minded AI-Driven Strategy-Minded

Currently pursuing my Master's in TESOL at the University of Edinburgh, I came here after finishing my undergraduate degree in English at Lishui University — though English wasn't where I started. I began in sociology, and switched somewhere along the way, chasing whatever it was I was curious about at the time.

My interest in AI started in my second semester here, after I kept coming across videos of things people had built with AI and couldn't stop wondering how. That curiosity led me down the rabbit hole of vibe coding — clumsily at first, until a data analysis project I built actually worked, and something clicked. Now I'm tinkering with small projects, like a demo that analyzes singing videos to suggest better ways to film for TikTok. It's a departure from my academic background, but the kind I've learned to trust — I'm working toward becoming an AI product manager, one small project at a time.
